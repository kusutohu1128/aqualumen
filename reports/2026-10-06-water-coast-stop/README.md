## What changed the next action

The prior actual coast attribution selected eight dark/proxy versus adjacent
bright/opaque-hit pairs. The new passive witnesses capture the original
refraction invocation's last candidate, depth, ray, origin, travel, residual and
exit reason, without a second trace or additional depth lookup.

In actual Iris/NVIDIA, all eight dark points reach iteration8 and fail the
unchanged residual gate. All eight adjacent bright points succeed. These are
known water producers, not unknown bathymetry, and every selected SSR path is
missing (-1). None of these eight misses stops at viewport, foreground-bank,
above-surface, nonpositive-projection or sky guards. This narrows the observed
failure mode but does not independently prove a correct physical receiver exists.

Only the upper-surface iteration budget was then changed9->32 in a NEW TEST ZIP.
All guard predicates and tolerances remain, underwater still uses8 iterations,
and only one ZIP entry differs. At iteration31 **all eight selected dark points
still fail**, while all eight neighboring positive controls still succeed.
Increasing the budget alone therefore did not recover any selected receiver.
Do not ship this ablation or relax a guard/tolerance to manufacture convergence.
The next useful investigation is first-visible terrain/source geometry and
depth-cell oscillation, not simply another larger iteration count.
