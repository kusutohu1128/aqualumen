## New vertex-input module, not a producer

`ReceiverVertex.capture` owns exactly four pre-encoder Sodium vertex records and
prepares the separate-AO color/light codes used by the pinned Iris encoder.
Any applied Sodium tint is already in mesher color; it is not multiplied again.
Color alpha is8-bit AO (`0.5` becomes127, not128). Smoothed light retains its raw
8-bit component precision, then the pinned8..248 encoded clamp; it is not reduced
to neighboring0..15 block light. Invalid AO/component bits/coordinates/UV and
unsupported separate-AO or input shape stay unready. Caller-array reuse cannot
mutate the owned result. Raw geometry/UV are explicitly **pre-encoder**, not
claimed quantized GPU positions, sampling coordinates or face interpolation.

Four public behavioral RED->GREEN increments are frozen individually, along
with supplemental ownership/clamp controls. Tests call the actual installed
Sodium `ColorABGR.withAlpha` as a reference; the synthetic test inputs are not
actual Minecraft mesher records. No module collaborators are mocked.

`vertex-boundary` retains installed Iris1.8.8/Sodium0.6.9 bytecode observations,
JAR identities and actual ordinary-pack `separateAo=true` property. The traced
path is BlockRenderer's prepared vertex/color/AO/light arrays, the Iris XHFP
encoder and SodiumTransformer's gl_Color/lightmap mapping. Actual mesh events,
encoded output buffers, draw input/iris_LightmapTextureMatrix, filtered UV/LOD,
face ownership, live snapshot epoch and shaded source are **not** captured here.
No Mixin, dynamic sampler, GL upload or normal installed Mod is added.
