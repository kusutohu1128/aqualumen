## What did not improve

The circular/distorted sky window, deep folded rim/holes and dark exterior remain
in the three viewed images. Level one interpolates already displaced vertices;
it cannot add geometric wave detail. All selected reflection samples are misses.
No optical-normal, IOR, transport, exposure or physical fluid change was made.
No natural visual quality, motion, shipping performance or FPS floor is accepted.
Measured roughly 118.4–118.6 FPS at 900p with cap120 is only a warmed stationary
diagnostic result, not a final-performance promise.

`iris-near-raw-failed.json` intentionally retains its original filename. Its data
were valid: the first auditor incorrectly required interpolated C12 alpha to
equal exactly 1. The retained RED led to an eight-binary32-epsilon selected-point
guard; unchanged raw data then passed. This was an auditor-contract correction,
not a shader failure or a universal floating-point error bound.
