six actual programs, four immutable ZIP source chains, 300 real-output maps,

maps, 90 typed guard queries, 160 attached-stage digests and 369 comparison gates.

are hashed in `result.json`.

zero-cache/guard0 controls retain defaults. The maximum old/stable positive
central C0 difference is 0.004150390625, within the predeclared absolute2e-6 plus
relative2e-4 gate; this is an empirical interior regression, not a physical oracle.

failures, all category3. All 5,202 saved first-target records are replayed through
the frozen public `analyze_record`: positive discriminant, 80/120-digit agreement,
and `SUPPORTED_REPLAY_ACCEPTED`. Their old/stable records are identical. This
fixture does **not** reproduce the supported numerical compound-gate rejection

reflected-hemisphere gate; the inverse-node reference does not qualify that gate.

classify every later node. Input C12 is not TES, and slots5–7 are not shader-output
evidence. No forced critical geometry, all48-cause, native pixel-AA, Iris, natural
appearance, motion, energy, FPS-floor or shipping acceptance is claimed.

the first successful replay before adding per-comparison/max-passive reporting;
