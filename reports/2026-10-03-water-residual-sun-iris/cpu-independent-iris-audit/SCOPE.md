# Independent saved Iris audit

This NEW audit reads only public experiment artifacts, packs, source and frame CSVs. It does not read launcher/account logs, execute GL, control clients or change root-owned artifacts. All six root capture-completion notifications are required before running the full audit and publishing its result.

The script independently recomputes mean/median/nearest-rank p95 and 1000/mean FPS from every positive nanosecond CSV interval. Consecutive indices, ns/ms text agreement, recorded frame count/seconds/FPS and tails are verified against saved summaries. It never drops a slow frame or substitutes a summary for raw CSV.

Exact public pack/source recovery and raw-binding protocol are checked using the read-only protocol dependency, whose hash is recorded. Independent selected WNS/covariance/coefficient/TES counts and arithmetic are recomputed from actual role values. A scalar-int guard1, original flags15, linked stages or a screenshot alone are not new-feature application. Enabled1 must retain the predeclared four-applied-selected-point checkpoint; failures remain negative with fixed coordinates unchanged.

One actual near stage attachment observation exists per separately launched client. Deep/tilt fresh guard program IDs are compared with that client observation. No copied stage artifact, per-view stage query, exclusive-water link, draw/fetch or invocation-count proof is fabricated. Separate guard/FINAL/frame data are not PNG pixel-causality proof; FINAL has no capturedUtc field, so independent post-terminal timestamp evidence exists for guard and near stage only, not FINAL.

Timing is a warmed fixed-view Minecraft render-tail interval measurement at 1600x900, cap120, Vsyncfalse, warm10s/sample5s. Fresh authorization filesystem/runtime/pack hashing remains enabled in both modes per source, and its overhead is included rather than subtracted. This is not pure GPU time, uncapped/1440p throughput, motion performance, or a general 60FPS floor. The registered per-frame supplier source is not a continuous readback of every timing frame's actual integer value.

Saved options at control, between clients and at treatment are compared and serialize fov0.0; no angle is inferred. Actual camera/eye, world time and dimensions are captured; weather clear/frozen-cycle is source-command intent because fresh rain/thunder uniforms are not exported. The explicit no-terrain-placement scene record/source is verified. Root alone inspects PNG appearance; this auditor checks only PNG signature/IHDR dimensions and hashes, without Pillow or image editing.

No pixel-AA equivalence, arbitrary covariance/rim/critical/visibility, physical-model, visual, motion, energy/reciprocity or shipping acceptance is implied. Rejection counts are observed but their underlying predicate cause is not exported here.
