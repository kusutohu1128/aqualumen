# Land shadow design rubric

Score0to2 per item. Reject a design that fails preservation or binding safety.

1. Preserves every ordinary archive payload except two opaque wrapper entries and added libraries. Legacy water/compute/composite/caster programs and shared state remain unchanged.
2. Uses a sampler/state contract compatible with pinned Iris1.8.8, or labels unverified new runtime binding rather than blessing it.
3. Addresses distance/distortion-dependent softness and slope/self-shadowing with coherent metric/receiver-plane geometry rather than larger arbitrary offsets.
4. Gives a bounded pure data shape and small opt-in boundary, with no copied lighting implementation or broad pipeline rewrite.
5. Has numerical positive and adversarial tests on real generated shaders, especially sloped receiver, floating contact, distortion centre/edge, map bounds and legacy preservation.
6. Produces a usable preview without interrupting the running client or mislabeling native timings as game FPS.
